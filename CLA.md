### Why a CLA and not only the DCO?
The Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) certifies provenance but does not grant the Licensor the right to **relicense** contributions under commercial terms. The commercial licensing program ([COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md)) requires that right, so a CLA is required. Contributors are **also** asked to add a `Signed-off-by` line (DCO) for provenance tracking.
