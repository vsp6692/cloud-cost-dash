# Legal and Licensing Documentation

> All documents here and the root licensing files are **proposed drafts for review by qualified legal counsel**. None are final or legal advice.

CloudLedger uses a **source-available** model (not OSI open source):
- **Free**: download, inspect, test, self-host, modify, and use internally, including in production, by individuals, startups and enterprises. No user, account or resource limits.
- **Commercial license required**: resale, hosting or managed services for third parties (paid or free), white-labeling, embedding in commercially distributed products, derivative products, and commercial redistribution or sublicensing.

## Key distinction used throughout
| Internal commercial **use** (free) | Commercial **redistribution or third-party services** (licensed) |
|---|---|
| A business uses CloudLedger to manage **its own and its affiliates'** cloud costs, in production, at any scale | A business provides CloudLedger, or its functionality, **to others**: resale, SaaS, MSP, OEM, white-label, derivative products |
