# CloudLedger Commercial Licensing

> **PROPOSED FRAMEWORK. Requires legal review.** This page describes when a commercial license is needed and how the program is intended to work. It is not an offer, contract or legal advice. Actual terms are set only in a signed written agreement with the Licensor.

## Do I need a commercial license?

**Internal use is free.** A commercial license is **not** required to download, inspect, test, self-host, modify or run CloudLedger in production to manage costs that **you or your affiliates** incur. That holds for individuals, startups and large enterprises alike. There are **no user, account, resource, tenant or deployment limits** on internal use, and no license keys.

A commercial license **is** required for **commercial distribution or providing CloudLedger to third parties**, including:

| Activity | Commercial license needed? |
|---|---|
| Running CloudLedger in production for your own company's AWS/GCP/Azure costs | **No** |
| Internal chargeback to your own business units or affiliates | **No** |
| A consultant operating CloudLedger **inside a client's environment, for that client only** | **No** (the client is the licensee; the consultant is an Authorized Contractor) |
| Sending CloudLedger-generated reports or exports to your customers, auditors or board | **No** |
| Contributing code, connectors or plugins to the project | **No** (CLA required) |
| Reselling CloudLedger or bundling it for sale | **Yes** |
| Hosting CloudLedger as a SaaS or managed service for external customers, **paid or free** | **Yes** |
| An MSP or FinOps service provider running CloudLedger to manage **its customers'** cloud costs | **Yes** |
| White-labeling or rebranding CloudLedger for distribution or service to others | **Yes** |
| Embedding CloudLedger (or a substantial part) into a commercially distributed product or appliance | **Yes** |
| Building and selling a derivative product substantially based on CloudLedger source code | **Yes** |
| Sublicensing or commercially redistributing CloudLedger | **Yes** |

When in doubt, read the [License FAQ](LICENSE_FAQ.md) or contact the Licensor.

## Proposed commercial license types

These are proposals for the Licensor and counsel to finalize. Names, scope and pricing are not set.

| Type | For | Typical grants |
|---|---|---|
| **SaaS / Managed Service Provider License** | SaaS operators, MSPs, FinOps service providers, cloud resellers | right to operate CloudLedger for third-party customers; multi-customer tenancy; optional co-branding |
| **OEM / Embedded License** | vendors embedding CloudLedger in their products or appliances | redistribution in object code as part of the licensee's product; sublicensing to end customers under approved terms |
| **Reseller / Distribution License** | resellers and system integrators | resale of CloudLedger or support packages within a territory |
| **White-label License** | partners presenting CloudLedger under their own brand | rebranding rights; trademark carve-outs |

Common terms to be defined by counsel: scope and field of use, territory, term and renewal, fees (flat, revenue share or per-customer), attribution and branding, trademark rights, source-modification rights, confidentiality, warranty, indemnity, liability caps, security obligations, audit rights, termination, and handling of contributions back to the project.

## Principles the Licensor commits to (proposed)

1. **Internal use stays free.** Commercial licensing will never introduce artificial limits, license keys or feature gating on internal use of the source-available edition.
2. **One codebase.** Commercial licensees receive the same Software. The license changes the permitted *use*, not the features.
3. **Written authorization only.** No commercial right is granted implicitly, through conduct, or through contribution. Only a written agreement signed by the Licensor grants these rights.
4. **The Licensor retains control** over commercial licensing, partner authorization and use of the CloudLedger trademarks.
