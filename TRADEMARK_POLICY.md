## Goals
- Users can trust that anything called "CloudLedger" comes from, or is authorized by, the Licensor.
- The community can accurately refer to the project.

## Permitted without asking
- **Referring to the project accurately**: "We use CloudLedger", "Plugin for CloudLedger", "Compatible with CloudLedger", "CloudLedger connector for Example Cloud".
- **Internal deployments**: using the unmodified name and logo in your internal self-hosted deployment.
- **Unmodified source shares**: public forks for contribution under LICENSE §3(d), provided the fork's README states it is an unofficial fork.
- **Community content**: articles, talks, tutorials and meetups about CloudLedger, provided they don't imply endorsement.

## Requires written permission
- Using the Marks in the name of a **product, service, company, domain or social account** (e.g. "CloudLedger Cloud", "cloudledger-hosting.com").
- Any **hosted, managed, white-label, OEM or resale** offering. These require a commercial license ([COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md)), which will specify branding rights.
- **Modified distributions** that are distributed or presented to third parties under the CloudLedger name.
- Merchandise and logos in materials that could imply sponsorship.

## Never permitted
- Implying the Licensor endorses or certifies your product, service or fork without written agreement.
- Using confusingly similar names or logos.
- Calling a modified version "CloudLedger" in a way that suggests it is the official release.

## Plugins and connectors
Out-of-tree plugins may use descriptive phrasing such as "**for CloudLedger**" or "**CloudLedger-compatible**". They must not use "CloudLedger" as the first word of the plugin name unless published by the Licensor, and must not suggest official status unless listed as certified. A certification program for plugins that pass the compatibility suite may be defined in Phase 37.
